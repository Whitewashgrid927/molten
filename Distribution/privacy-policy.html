Molten Privacy

Updated October 23, 2026

Molten is an offline interactive graphics app developed by the Molten contributors. The app has no account, advertising, analytics, tracking, or network connection. The developer does not collect data through the app.

Your images and audio

Molten accesses files that you select in a file dialog or drop into the app. Images are processed locally for the glass effect and audio is played locally in the koi turntable. Exported images are saved to a location you choose. Your files are never uploaded.

Local preferences

Scene, rendering quality, and frame-rate preferences are stored on your Mac. Animation and performance timing is processed locally. Molten does not request microphone, camera, screen-recording, or Accessibility access.

Apple services

Apple operates App Store and TestFlight downloads and may provide crash reports or beta feedback according to your Apple settings. Those services are governed by Apple’s privacy policy.

Your control

You can quit Molten to end file access and processing. You control saved exports using Finder. Local preferences remain on your Mac until you remove them.
